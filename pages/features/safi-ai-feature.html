Questions.Answered.

Can Safi AI change my data?

No. Safi AI is read-only. If you ask it to create, edit or delete something, it tells you where to do it in SafiTrack instead.

What can my reps see?

The same as they can in the app. Reps get answers about their own tasks, deals and visits. Managers can ask about the whole team.

Which AI model does it use?

Google Gemini. Requests go through SafiTrack's servers, which hold the key, so nothing sensitive is exposed in the browser.

Do I need to set anything up?

No. Visit summaries and lead scores are added automatically when a visit is logged, and you can open the assistant from Ask Safi at the top of the app.
